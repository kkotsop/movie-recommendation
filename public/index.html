How does “Log in with Netflix” work?

A normal browser window opens on netflix.com. You sign in and pick your profile there — SwipeFlick never sees your password. It then reads your Viewing activity page and closes the window. Everything stays on this computer.

Prefer not to? On netflix.com go to Account → Profiles → (your profile) → Viewing activity → Download all, then upload the NetflixViewingHistory.csv file here.

By swiping you agree to find something great to watch tonight.
