About & credits

Your data

Swipeflix never asks for, sees or stores your Netflix email or password. Your viewing history and swipes stay on your own device and recommendations are worked out there, so none of it is sent to us. Moving your data to another device, if you choose to, is the only time it travels. “Reset everything” in Profile deletes it.

Catalog, posters and trending

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Titles, posters, cast, keywords and what is trending come from The Movie Database.

Ratings

IMDb ratings come from IMDb's non-commercial datasets, for personal use.

Swipeflix is not affiliated with Netflix, IMDb or TMDB.
