How does syncing work?

You save a bookmark once. When you tap it on netflix.com, it reads your Viewing activity in your own signed-in browser and brings the list back here. It stays on this phone.

Your Netflix password is never asked for, seen or stored by Swipeflix. You sign in on netflix.com itself. Your viewing activity is read and kept on this phone only: it is never sent to us. “Reset everything” in Profile deletes it.

By swiping you agree to find something great to watch tonight.

Your data

Swipeflix never asks for, sees or stores your Netflix email or password. Your viewing activity and swipes are saved on your own device and recommendations are worked out there too, so none of it is sent to us. The one exception: names of watched titles we don't recognise are looked up on TMDB through our server (names only, no dates and nothing that identifies you). “Reset everything” in Profile deletes your data.

Swipeflix is not affiliated with Netflix, IMDb or TMDB.

Sync from Netflix

  1. Save the bookmark. Tap Copy bookmark, then add any page as a bookmark and paste this into its address. On iPhone: Safari → Share → Add Bookmark, then edit it and paste. On Android: Chrome → ⋮ → ☆, then edit it and paste.
  2. Open Netflix. Tap Open viewing activity, sign in and pick your profile.
  3. Tap the bookmark. In the address bar, type its name and choose it (iPhone: open bookmarks and tap it). It reads your history and brings you back here. Using the iPhone home-screen app? Afterwards open Profile → Move your data to carry it into the app.
Open viewing activity

We don't store your Netflix login. The bookmark runs inside your own browser, where you are already signed in to Netflix. It reads only your viewing list (titles and dates) and hands it to Swipeflix, which keeps it on this phone and never sends it anywhere. Your email and password never leave netflix.com, and the app can't see them. “Reset everything” in Profile deletes the list.
